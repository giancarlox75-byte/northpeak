Meet Kevin

Michigan Made.
Future Focused.

I'm Kevin Reece. I was born and raised in Flint, and I grew up all over this state, from Flint to Ann Arbor to Troy. I've seen what Michigan families need, and I know what this state is capable of.

I studied how government works and how laws get made. I've worked alongside people getting legal help they couldn't otherwise afford, and inside the legislative process in Lansing. Now I want to put that to work making Michigan a stronger place for all of us.

The Movement

Building a
Stronger Michigan.

Team Reece is a place for ideas, community, and the future we're building together. It starts with a simple belief: Michigan should stay at the top, leading in technology and in how we take care of each other.

That means AI that helps people grow instead of replacing them, with real oversight whenever it touches our rights. Homes young families can afford. Strong public schools and teachers who are valued. Better jobs with better pay. And a government that's open about how it spends your money.

This campaign is built from the ground up, one conversation, one neighborhood, one county at a time. This is just the beginning. Follow along. Get involved. Be part of what's next.

Questions

Straight Answers.

Who is Kevin Reece?
Kevin was born and raised in Flint and graduated from Carman-Ainsworth High School. He earned a political science degree from Western Michigan University, focused on public policy and public law, with a minor in technology innovation. He has interned with Legal Aid of Western Michigan and served as a legislative and policy intern in the Michigan House.
What is Team Reece?
Team Reece is a grassroots movement to build a stronger Michigan: affordable homes and everyday costs, AI that works for people, strong public schools, better-paying jobs, and open government. It's a home for anyone who wants leaders who actually answer to the people they serve.
Where does Kevin stand on AI?
AI should help people grow, not replace them. Kevin wants every Michigander to have the training to use AI in their careers, support for workers when automation hits their jobs, and real government oversight whenever AI or surveillance technology touches people's rights. That includes license-plate camera networks like Flock and facial recognition. Michigan can lead on technology and protect our rights at the same time.
What office is Reece running for?
The official campaign announcement is coming soon. Join the team to be the first to know. Right now we're focused on listening to Michiganders and building the organization that wins.
I've never volunteered before. Can I still help?
Absolutely, most of our team is new to this. We run free trainings, pair new volunteers with experienced organizers, and there's work for every comfort level, including from your couch.
How can I get Reece to come to my town?
Sign up to host an event in the Join form, or email us. The listening tour is going to every region of the state, and community invitations move you up the list.
Where does my donation go?
Directly into organizing: events, volunteer tools, signs, literature, and digital outreach. Contributions are reported as required by law, and we'll publish plain-language spending updates.
